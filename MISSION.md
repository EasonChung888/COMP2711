# Mission: COMP2711 Logic

## Why
Build enough real understanding of COMP2711 Logic in two focused days to read formal notation without panic, choose a valid method, and earn reliable marks on lecture-level and past-paper questions.

## Success looks like
- Translate between English and propositional or predicate logic accurately.
- Simplify propositions and justify each equivalence step.
- Construct valid propositional and predicate inference chains.
- Choose and write a direct, contrapositive, contradiction, or biconditional proof.
- Score at least 14/20 on the unseen final Logic mini-mock.

## Constraints
- Start from zero assumed prior knowledge.
- Study for two days at roughly two to three hours per day.
- Use English for all teaching and assessment content.
- Prefer detailed explanation and worked examples over dense practice sets.
- Follow lectures L01-L03 and verified COMP2711 past-paper difficulty.

## Out of scope
- Full formal semantics, proof theory, and automated theorem proving.
- Extended drilling of null quantification, unique existence, NAND, or advanced normal forms beyond the optional stretch sections.
- COMP2711 topics outside the Logic lecture group.
