Problem 5: [10 pts]
(a) Use the Euclidean algorithm to find gcd(15, 221). Show the steps.
(b) Find x ∈ Z such that 15x ≡ 2 (mod 221). Show your workings.
221
Solution: (a) gcd(221,15):
221 = 15·14+11
15 = 11·1+4
11 = 4·2+3
4 = 3·1+1
3 = 1·3+0
So, gcd(221,15) = 1.
(a) Rewriting:
11 = 221−15·14
4 = 15−11·1
3 = 11−4·2
1 = 4−3·1
Substituting:
1 = 4−(11−4·2)·1
= 4·3−11·1
= (15−11·1)·3−11·1
= 15·3−11·4
= 15·3−(221−15·14)·4
= 15·59−221·4
Therefore, the inverse of 15 modulo 221 is 59.
15−1 ·15x ≡ 15−1 ·2 (mod 221).
x ≡ 59·2 ≡ 118 (mod 221).
