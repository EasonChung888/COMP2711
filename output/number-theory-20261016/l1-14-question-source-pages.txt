Problem 3: [10 pts] Show that there is no largest prime number.
Solution : We prove this by contradiction.
Suppose there is a largest prime number n. Note that n! + 1 has no factors
between 1 and n. Thus, if all primes were n or less, n! + 1 would have no
prime factors, and it would be prime itself. Therefore, n!+1 is a prime number
greater than n, which is a contradiction.
