# STEM-2015-MID-P07 - Shared Problem Prompt

## Shared printed wording

You are going to distribute $kn$ cash coupons to $kn$ members in your company such that each member will get one cash coupon, where $k,n\ge1$. The values of the $kn$ cash coupons are distinct. Suppose there are $n$ ranks of seniority in the company, and there are exactly $k$ members at each rank. If a more senior member receives a cash coupon worth less than the cash coupon given to a more junior member, you will receive a complaint.
