▪ A machine that inserts letters into envelopes goes
  haywire and inserts letters randomly into envelopes. In
  a group of 100 letters, how many ways if
a) No letter is put into the correct envelope?
Answer: 𝐷100
b) Exactly 1 letter is put into the correct envelope?
Answer: C(100,1)∙𝐷99 ways to choose the letter correctly
stuffed. 𝐷99 ways to insert the remaining 99 letters all
incorrectly.
c) Exactly 98 letters are put into the correct envelopes?
Answer: C(100,98)=C(100,2). Choose two letters to be
misplaced and there is only one way to misplace them.
