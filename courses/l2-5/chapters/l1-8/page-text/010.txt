    Exercise on Employees and Tasks
▪   How many ways are there to assign 6 different tasks to 3 different employees if every
    employee is assigned at least one task?
▪   How many assignments would be possible if there were no constraint?
▪   36=729, 3 options for 1st task, 3 options for 2nd,…(permutations with repetition)
▪   Wrong Solution for constrained version:
1.   Step 1: Choose the first 3 tasks (C(6,3)) and assign them to 3 different employees:
     C(6,3)3!=P(6,3). Each employee is assigned a task
2.   Step 2: Assign the remaining 3 tasks to any employee: 33
▪   Total number of ways:  C(6,3)3!33=20627=3240
▪   Mistake: Overcounting. The correct solution should be less than 729.
▪   Assume that in an assignment x, Step 1 one assigns task 1 to employee 1 and Step 2
    assigns task 2 to employee 1.
▪   Assume another assignment x', which is identical to x (all tasks are assigned to the
    same employees), but task 2 is assigned to employee 1 at Step 1, and task 1 is
    assigned to employee 1 at Step 2.
▪    x and x' are counted as distinct solutions, whereas they are equivalent.
