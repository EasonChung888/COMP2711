    Number of Permutations that are not Derangements

▪  Assuming permutations of n numbers 1,2,..,i,..,n, we will count the
   number of permutations that are not derangements.
▪  Let Pi be the set of permutations, where i remains in its initial position
   (these permutations are not derangements).
▪  What should I compute?
▪  |P1P2…  Pi… Pn| by inclusion - exclusion
▪  What is the value of |Pi|?
▪  (n-1)! since i must remain in the same position, I can only permute the
   remaining n-1 numbers.
▪  Which permutations belong to Pi Pj?
▪  Permutations that leave i and j in their original positions.
▪  What is the value of |Pi Pj|?
▪  (n-2)!
▪  What is the value of |P1 P2...Pr| and every intersection of r sets?
▪  (n-r)!
