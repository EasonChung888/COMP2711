   Derangement


▪ A derangement is a permutation of objects that leaves
  no object in its original position.
▪ Example
  The permutation 21453 is a derangement of 12345
  because no number is left in its original position.
  However, 21543 is not a derangement of 12345
  because this permutation leaves 4 unchanged.
▪ Theorem
  The number of derangements of a set with           𝑛 elements
  is
            𝐷   = 𝑛! 1 −  1 +  1 −  1 + ⋯   −1  𝑛 1
              𝑛           1!   2!  3!             𝑛!
