    Exercise on Employees and Tasks
▪  How many ways are there to assign 5 distinct tasks to 4 different
   people such that every person has at least one task assigned?
▪  Solution 1: This is the number of surjective functions from a set of
   5 elements to a set of 4 elements: 45-C(4,1)35 + C(4,2)25 - C(4,3)15
   =1024-972+192-4 = 240
▪  Solution 2: A person must be assigned 2 tasks, and the rest 1 task
   each. Select 2 tasks and assign them to some person in
   C(5,2)x4=40 ways. Then assign the remaining tasks to 3 persons
   in 3!=6 ways. Total is C(5,2)x4 x3!=240
▪  Solution 3: Select 4 tasks and assign them to 4 persons in
   C(5,4)x4!=5x24=120 ways. Then, we assign the remaining task to
   one of the 4 users in 4 ways. This solution (C(5,4)x4!x4=480)
   double counts for the user that has 2 tasks (e.g., (task1, task2)
   and (task2,task1) are counted as 2 assignments). To avoid this
   problem we divide by 2, to get the answer C(5,4)x4!x2=240.
