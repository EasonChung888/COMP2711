▪  Remark: Using set builders may lead to           paradoxes
▪  A village has a single male barber who follows a strict rule: he
   shaves all men who       do not shave themselves         , and only those men.
▪  Russel's paradox:      Who shaves the barber?
▪  If the barber shaves himself:         Then he breaks rule, because he is
   only supposed to shave men who do not shave themselves.
▪  If the barber does not shave himself:            Then he must be shaved by
   the barber (i.e., by himself), breaking the rule that he shaves
   everyone who doesn't shave themselves.
▪  Leads to axiomatic set theory. In this course we study “            naïve set
   theory”
