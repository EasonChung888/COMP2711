Example: Insertion Sort

       Insertion-Sort(𝐴):
       for  𝑗←  2to  𝑛  do
            𝑘𝑒𝑦 ← 𝐴[𝑗]
            𝑖←  𝑗− 1
            while 𝑖  ≥ 1 and 𝐴[𝑖]  > 𝑘𝑒𝑦  do
                 𝐴[𝑖+ 1] ← 𝐴[𝑖]
                 𝑖←  𝑖− 1
            endwhile
            𝐴 𝑖+ 1  ← 𝑘𝑒𝑦
       endfor
