  Mistakes in Proofs

What is wrong with this famous supposed “proof” that 1 = 2?

Let a and b be two equal positive integers.
1. a = b                             Given
2. a2 = ab                           Multiply both sides of (1) by a
3. a2 − b2 = ab − b2                 Subtract b2 from both sides of (2)
4. (a − b)(a + b) = b(a − b)         Factor both sides of (3)
5. a + b = b                         Divide both sides of (4) by a − b
6. 2b = b                            Replace a by b in (5) because a = b
7. 2 = 1                             Divide both sides of (6) by b

Error:  Step  5  where    we   divided   both   sides   by  (a −   b). The   error  is
   that (a − b) equals zero. Division of both sides of             an equation by
   the same quantity is valid as long as this quantity is not zero.
