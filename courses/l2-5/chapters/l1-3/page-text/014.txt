 Example 1 of Fallacy (invalid argument)
▪  Is the argument valid:
"if you were a good student, you are rich".
"You are rich"
Therefore: "You were a good student"
▪  p: "You were a good student "
▪  q: "You are rich"
                 p →  q
                 q
                 p
▪  Wrong   because    p  →  q  is not  equivalent   to  q →   p. p  =  F, q  = T
   makes the premises true, but the conclusion false.
