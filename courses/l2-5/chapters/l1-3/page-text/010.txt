Example on Application of Resolution rule

▪ Given the hypotheses “it is not snowing or Jasmine is
  skiing” and “it is snowing or Bart is playing hockey”
  draw a conclusion.
▪ s: it is snowing
▪ k: Jasmine is skiing
▪ h: Bart is playing hockey
▪ Solution
                s  ∨ k
               s  ∨ h
               k ∨ h    (by resolution)
“Jasmine is skiing or Bart is playing hockey”
