Example on (Universal) Modus Ponens

▪  Show that the premises “all men must die” and “John Snow is a
   man” imply the conclusion “John Snow must die”, using the
   following predicates.
    ▪  𝑃(𝑥): 𝑥 is a man
    ▪  𝐶(𝑥): 𝑥 must die
▪  Solution
1.  ∀x  (P(x) →  C(x))                      Premise
2.   P(John Snow)                            Premise
3.  P(John Snow) →     C(John Snow)  1 Universal instantiation
4.  C(John Snow)                            2,3 Modus ponens
Combining 3. and 4. into a single step
1.  ∀x  (P(x) →  C(x))                      Premise
2.   P(John Snow)                            Premise
3. C(John Snow)  1,2 Universal Modus ponens
