 Proof by Contradiction for
 Conditional Statement (cont’d)

▪ We can rewrite a proof by contraposition of a
  conditional statement p→q as a proof by
  contradiction.
▪ In a proof by contraposition, we assume that ¬q is true
  and then show that ¬p must also be true.
▪ To rewrite as a proof by contradiction, we suppose
  that both p and ¬q are true. Then, we use the steps
  from the proof of ¬q     →  ¬p to show that ¬p is true.
  This leads to the contradiction p ^ ¬p, completing the
  proof.
