 Limitation of Direct Proofs


▪ Direct proofs are useful but attempts at direct proofs
  sometimes lead to dead ends. There are other proof
  techniques.

   Proofs that are not direct     proofs, i.e., they do not start
  with   the  hypothesis    and   end   with  the  conclusion,    are
  called indirect proofs.

    We will consider several types of indirect proofs.
