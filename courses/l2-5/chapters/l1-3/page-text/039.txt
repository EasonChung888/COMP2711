 Example of rewriting a proof by contraposition of a
 conditional statement p→q as a proof by contradiction

▪ “If integer 3n+2 is odd, then n is odd.”
▪ P: Odd(3n+2) Q: Odd(n), P→Q
▪ Proof by contraposition: ¬Q→¬P
▪ Assume     ¬Q,  i.e., even   n=2k. Then,     I will prove  ¬P,  i.e.,
  even 3n+2
▪ 3n+2 = 3(2k)+2 = 6k+2 = 2(3k+1)=2k'
▪ Proof by contradiction:
▪ Assume      ¬Q,   i.e., even   n=2k    and   P,  i.e., odd    3n+2.
  Then, I will reach a contradiction.
▪ 3n+2=3(2k)+2=6k+2=2(3k+1)=2k'.              3n+2   is even,   which
  contradicts the assumption that 3n+2 is odd.
