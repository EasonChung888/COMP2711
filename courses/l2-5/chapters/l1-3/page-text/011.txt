  Example on application of Resolution,
  Simplification rules

▪  Show that the hypotheses (p ^ q) ∨ r and r→s imply
   the conclusion p ∨      s.
▪  Solution
      1. (p ^ q) ∨ r          Premise
     2.  r →   s                Premise
      3.   r ∨ s              2, equivalence
      4. (p ^ q) ∨  s          1,3 resolution
      5. (p∨s)^(q∨s)       4, equivalence
      6.  p ∨ s                 5, simplification
