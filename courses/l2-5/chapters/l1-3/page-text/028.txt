  Exercise 1 on Direct Proof



▪  Definition: An integer a is a perfect square if there is an integer b
   such that a = b2

▪  Give a  direct  proof of:   “if m and n    are both perfect squares,       then
   mn is also a perfect square”
▪  Assume     the   hypothesis    is  true:  m=s2    and   n=t2,  where    s,t  are
   integers.
▪  We must show that mn is a perfect square.
▪  mn = s2t2 = (st)2. Thus, mn is a perfect square (of s•t).
