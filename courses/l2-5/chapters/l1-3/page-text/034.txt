Exercise on Proof by Contraposition

▪  Prove   that if n  is an  integer   and  n2  is even,  then   n  is
   even.
▪  P(n): n2 is even, Q(n): n is even
▪  Proof by Contraposition: ¬Q(n)  →         ¬P(n)
▪  Assume     ¬Q(n),  i.e., n  is odd.  Then    I will prove   ¬P(n),
   i.e., n2 must be odd.
▪  n  = 2k+1       n2 =  (2k+1)2   =  4k2+4k+1     =  2(2k2+2k)+1
   =2k' +1                                                  n2 odd
