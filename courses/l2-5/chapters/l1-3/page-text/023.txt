 Terminology on Proof Techniques

▪ Definition: An axiom is a statement that is assumed
  to be true.
▪ Definition: A theorem is a statement that can be
  shown to be true.
▪ Definition: A less important theorem that is helpful in
  the proof of other theorems is called a lemma.
▪ Definition: A proof is a valid argument that
  establishes the truth of a theorem. The statements
  used in a proof can include axioms, premises of the
  theorem, and previously proved theorems or lemmas.
  Using these ingredients and rules of inference, the
  final step of the proof establishes the truth of the
  statement being proved.
