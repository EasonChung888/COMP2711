 Discrete Probability = Counting

▪ In Discrete Probability the set of outcomes is finite or
  countably infinite
▪ Example 1
▪ A bag contains 4 blue balls and 5 red balls. What is
  the probability that a ball chosen from the bag is blue?
▪ 4/9
▪ Example 2
▪ What is the probability that when two dice are rolled,
  the sum of the numbers on the two dice is 7?
▪ By the product rule there are 62 = 36 possible
  outcomes. Six of these sum to 7. Hence, the
  probability of obtaining a 7 is 6/36 = 1/6.
