 The Birthday Problem

▪ Question
  What is the minimum number of people who need to
  be in a room so that there must be two people with
  the same birthday, assuming that there are 366 days
  in a year?
▪ Answer: 367 (pigeonhole principle)
▪ Question
  What is the minimum number of people who need to
  be in a room so that the probability that at least two of
  them have the same birthday is greater than 1/2,
  assuming that there are 366 days in a year, the
  birthdays of the people in the room are independent,
  and each birthday is equally likely?

                                                                 17
