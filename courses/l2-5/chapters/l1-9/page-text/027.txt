  Independence

▪  Definition
   Two events 𝐸 and 𝐹 are independent if and only if
             𝑝(𝐸 ∩  𝐹) =  𝑝(𝐸)𝑝(𝐹)
▪  How to check independence?
    ▪  Two unrelated events are independent; one does not affect the
       other.
    ▪  Use definition
▪  Examples
▪  If two cards are drawn     with  replacement from a deck of cards, the
   event of drawing a red card on the first trial and that of drawing a
   red card on the second trial are      independent.
▪  If two cards are drawn without replacement, the events are
   not  independent because there are fewer red cards for the second
   trial.
