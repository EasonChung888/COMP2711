  Exercise: Hatcheck Problem


▪  A new employee checks the hats of 𝑛 people at a restaurant,
   forgetting to put claim check numbers on the hats. When
   customers return for their hats, the checker gives them back hats
   randomly. What is the probability that no one receives the correct
   hat?
▪  Answer: Number of derangements/Total number of permutations
▪  Total number of permutations: 𝑛!
