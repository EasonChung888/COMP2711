 Example


▪ In a randomly generated bit string of length 4:
  𝐸: it begins with a 1
  𝐹: it contains an even number of 1s.
  Are 𝐸 and 𝐹 independent?
▪ Solution
   ▪  𝑝 𝐸  =  1/2.
   ▪  𝑝 𝐹  =  1/2.
   ▪  𝑝 𝐸 ∩ 𝐹   = |{1111, 1100, 1010, 1001}|/16=1/4.
   ▪  𝑝(𝐸 ∩ 𝐹)  = 𝑝(𝐸)𝑝(𝐹). So 𝐸 and 𝐹 are independent.
