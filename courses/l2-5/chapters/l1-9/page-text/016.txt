 Exercise on Birthdays

▪ What is the probability of a person having today,
  assuming 366 days per year and uniform distribution
  of birthdays?
▪ 1/366
▪ Probability of a person not having birthday today:
▪ 365/366
▪ Probability that none of n persons has birthday today:
▪ p(no birthday today)=(365/366)n.
▪ Probability that in a group of n people, at least one
  has birthday today?
▪ p(at least one birthday today)=1-(365/366)n
