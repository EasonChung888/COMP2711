 Exercise 2 on Child Gender
▪ Suppose a family have 3 children, each of which has
  equal probability to be a boy or a girl. Are these two
  events independent?
   ▪  𝐸: The family has children of both sexes
   ▪  𝐹: The family has at most one boy
▪ Solution:
   ▪  𝑝 𝐸  =  |𝐵𝐵𝐺,𝐵𝐺𝐵,𝐵𝐺𝐺,𝐺𝐵𝐵,𝐺𝐵𝐺,𝐺𝐺𝐵  |=  3
                           8                4
   ▪  𝑝 𝐹  =  |𝐵𝐺𝐺,𝐺𝐵𝐺,𝐺𝐺𝐵,𝐺𝐺𝐺  |=  1
                       8            2
   ▪  𝑝 𝐸 ∩  𝐹  = | 𝐵𝐺𝐺,𝐺𝐵𝐺,𝐺𝐺𝐵 | = 3
                         8          8
   ▪  𝑝(𝐸 ∩  𝐹) = 𝑝(𝐸)𝑝(𝐹). So they are independent
