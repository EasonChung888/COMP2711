     Exercise on Letter Arrangements
▪  What is the probability of the following events when we randomly select a
   permutation of the 26 lowercase letters of the English alphabet?
▪  The first 13 letters of the permutation are in alphabetical order.
▪  There are 13! possible permutations of the first 13 letters and only 1 has
   them in alphabetical order. Thus, p=1/13!. Alternatively, since the first 13
   letters are in order, we can only permute the remaining 13:
   p=P(26,13)/26!=1/13!
▪  The first letter of the permutation is A and the last letter is Z.
▪  Once I fix the first and the last letter, there are 24! permutations for the
   rest. Thus, p=24!/26!
▪  A and Z are next to each other in the permutation.
▪  I consider A and Z as a block. Thus, the number of possible permutations is
   2x25! (2 because I need to select A or Z to be first). p= 2x25!/26!=1/13.
▪  A and Z are not next to each other in the permutation.
▪  p=12/13 (based on previous question).
▪  Z precedes A in the permutation.
▪  Z is equally likely to precede or follow A. Thus, p=1/2.
▪  Z precedes both A and B in the permutation.
▪  In 1/3 of permutations each of the 3 letters will be first. Thus, p=1/3.                11
