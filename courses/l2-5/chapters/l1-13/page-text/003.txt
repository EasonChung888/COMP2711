 Greatest Common Divisor

▪ Definition
  Let 𝑎  and 𝑏   be integers, not both zero. The largest
  integer 𝑑   such that 𝑑    |𝑎  and also   𝑑 | 𝑏 is called the
  greatest common divisor of 𝑎          and 𝑏, denoted by
  gcd(𝑎,  𝑏).
▪ One can find the gcd       by prime factorizations
▪ Example
  120  =  23  ∙3 ∙5      500=  22∙53
   gcd(120,500) = 2min(3,2)   ∙3min(1,0)∙5min(1,3)=22∙30 ∙51= 20
