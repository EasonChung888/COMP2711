 The Chinese Remainder Theorem

▪ Theorem
  Let 𝑚1, 𝑚2, … ,𝑚𝑛  be pairwise relatively prime positive
  integers greater than one and 𝑎1,     𝑎2,…  ,𝑎𝑛 arbitrary
  integers. Then the system
                    𝑥 ≡  𝑎1(mod   𝑚1)
                    𝑥 ≡  𝑎2(mod  𝑚2)
                             …
                    𝑥 ≡ 𝑎𝑛 (mod   𝑚𝑛)
   has a unique solution modulo 𝑚        = 𝑚1𝑚2  ⋯  𝑚𝑛.
▪ Proof
  We’ll show that a solution exists by describing a way
  to construct the solution. (Uniqueness proof is left as
  exercise.)
