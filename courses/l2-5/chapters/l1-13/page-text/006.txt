▪ Theorem (Bézout’s      Theorem)
  If 𝑎 and 𝑏  are positive integers, then there exist
  integers 𝑠  and 𝑡  such that gcd(𝑎,   𝑏) = 𝑠𝑎 + 𝑡𝑏.
▪ Example
                  gcd(6,14)  = (−2)  ∙6 + 1 ∙14
▪ Instead of proving this theorem directly, we give an
  algorithm to find such 𝑠     and 𝑡.
