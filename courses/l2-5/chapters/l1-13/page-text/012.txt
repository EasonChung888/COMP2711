 Multiplicative Inverses are Unique

▪ Proof of uniqueness
   ▪ Suppose 𝑏,    𝑐 are both inverses of 𝑎, i.e.,
                  𝑎𝑏 ≡  1  mod   𝑚     (1)
                  𝑎𝑐 ≡  1  mod  𝑚        2
   ▪ Multiply both sides of      1  by 𝑐:
                      𝑎𝑏𝑐  ≡ 𝑐   mod  𝑚
   ▪ Multiply both sides of      2  by 𝑏:
                      𝑎𝑏𝑐  ≡ 𝑏   mod  𝑚
   ▪ So 𝑏  ≡  𝑐 (mod  𝑚), i.e., 𝑎 has a unique inverse in 𝐙𝑚      .
▪ The inverse of 𝑎    is written as 𝑎−1.
▪ Note: It’s also true that if gcd    𝑎,𝑚   ≠ 1, 𝑎−1  doesn’t
  exist. (Left as an exercise.)
