 Solving Congruences

▪ Corollary
  If gcd 𝑎,𝑚   = 1, the linear congruence
                       𝑎𝑥 ≡ 𝑏  mod  𝑚
  has a unique solution in 𝐙𝑚
▪ Proof
   ▪ Existence has already been proved by construction.
   ▪ Uniqueness: Suppose it has two solutions 𝑥1,       𝑥2:
                    𝑎𝑥1  ≡ 𝑏  mod  𝑚
                    𝑎𝑥2  ≡ 𝑏  mod  𝑚
     Multiply both by 𝑎−1:
                    𝑥1 ≡  𝑏𝑎−1  mod  𝑚
                    𝑥2 ≡  𝑏𝑎−1  mod  𝑚
     So, 𝑥1 ≡  𝑥2(mod  𝑚).
