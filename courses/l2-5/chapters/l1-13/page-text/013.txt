 Finding Inverses

▪ Given 𝑎,  𝑚  such that gcd    𝑎,𝑚   = 1, how to find the
  inverse of 𝑎   in 𝐙𝑚?
▪ Look at the proof of the previous theorem
   ▪  Use the extended Euclidean algorithm to find 𝑠          and  𝑡
      such that 𝑠𝑎   + 𝑡𝑚  = 1
   ▪  s mod m    is the multiplicative inverse of a in 𝐙𝑚.
▪ Example
  Find an inverse of 3     modulo 7
▪ Solution
  Using the extended Euclidean algorithm: 7           = 2∙3 + 1.
  we get −2∙3    + 1∙7 = 1, so 𝑠 =  −2.
  −2  mod  7 =  5 is the inverse of 3   in 𝐙7
