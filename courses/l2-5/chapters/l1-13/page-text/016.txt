 Solving Congruences

▪ Linear congruence
                        𝑎𝑥 ≡  𝑏(mod  𝑚)
▪ Given 𝑎, 𝑏, 𝑚, such that gcd   𝑎,𝑚   = 1. How to find 𝑥?
▪ Solution:
   ▪ Find 𝑎−1
   ▪ Multiply 𝑎−1  on both sides
▪ Example
   ▪ Solve 3𝑥  ≡  4(mod  7)
   ▪ Find 3−1  =  5
   ▪ Multiply 5  on both sides:
                 3−1 ⋅ 3𝑥 ≡ 3−1 ⋅ 4 mod  7
                        𝑥 ≡ 5   4 ≡  6(mod  7)
