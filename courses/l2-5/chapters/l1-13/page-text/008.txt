 Puzzle: Water Measuring

▪ Given
   ▪ Two bottles: one has
     volume of 500     ml and
     the other one 700 ml.
   ▪ Infinite water supply
▪ Goal: Get exactly 100 ml
  of water
▪ This follows exactly from
    100  = gcd  500,700
    = 3 ×  500 − 2 × 700
▪ Corollary: Any multiple of
  the gcd   can be obtained.
