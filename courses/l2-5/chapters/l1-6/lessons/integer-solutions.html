四种饼干选六块与非负整数解

四种饼干中选择六块，同类可重复、次序不计、供应充足。将六个星号分到四类，需要三条隔板，编码长度为九，因此

C(4+6−1,6)=C(9,6)=C(9,3)=84.C(4+6-1,6)=C(9,6)=C(9,3)=84.C(4+6−1,6)=C(9,6)=C(9,3)=84.

等价地，令 xix_ixi​ 为第 iii 类购买数量，计算

x1+x2+x3+x4=6,xi∈Z≥0x_1+x_2+x_3+x_4=6,\qquad x_i\in\mathbb Z_{\ge0}x1​+x2​+x3​+x4​=6,xi​∈Z≥0​

的解数。课件方程将 x2x_2x2​ 写了两次，按四种饼干的模型，第三项应为 x3x_3x3​。每个变量自然不超过 6，所以无需另行施加这个冗余上限。

若某类库存少于六块，84 就不一定正确。例如第 1 类只有五块，数量向量 (6,0,0,0)(6,0,0,0)(6,0,0,0) 被基础公式计入，却不可购买，必须排除。若该类仅两块，还要排除所有 x1≥3x_1\ge3x1​≥3 的解。

隔板法的基础结果允许任一类独占全部六块。库存上限会限制合法编码，需要另外处理，不可仅因为仍有四种饼干就继续使用 84。

这页将实际选择、盒子分配和非负整数方程统一成同一个模型，方便之后处理附加条件。

三个非负整数之和为五

计算

x1+x2+x3=5,x1,x2,x3∈Z≥0x_1+x_2+x_3=5,\qquad x_1,x_2,x_3\in\mathbb Z_{\ge0}x1​+x2​+x3​=5,x1​,x2​,x3​∈Z≥0​

的解数。变量有不同名称，因此 (5,0,0)(5,0,0)(5,0,0) 与 (0,5,0)(0,5,0)(0,5,0) 是不同解；允许零，所以不是只数正整数解。

将五个相同单位分到三个有标签的盒子，对应三个变量。使用五个星号和两条隔板，共七个位置。每段星号数就是该变量的值，所以

C(3+5−1,5)=C(7,5)=7!5!2!=21.C(3+5-1,5)=C(7,5)=\frac{7!}{5!2!}=21.C(3+5−1,5)=C(7,5)=5!2!7!​=21.

例如「||*」表示 (2,0,3)(2,0,3)(2,0,3)；开头「|」可以表示 x1=0x_1=0x1​=0，结尾「|」可以表示 x3=0x_3=0x3​=0。

也能独立检查：固定 x1=ax_1=ax1​=a，其中 a=0,…,5a=0,\ldots,5a=0,…,5，剩下 x2+x3=5−ax_2+x_3=5-ax2​+x3​=5−a 有 6−a6-a6−a 个解。因此总数为 6+5+4+3+2+1=216+5+4+3+2+1=216+5+4+3+2+1=21。

与选择三种饼干共五块完全等价：变量只记录每种买多少，盒子内无需排列相同单位。

有下限的整数解：先扣除保底数量

仍求 x1+x2+x3=5x_1+x_2+x_3=5x1​+x2​+x3​=5 的非负整数解，但额外要求 x3≥3x_3\ge3x3​≥3。饼干模型对应第三种至少买三块。

先为第三类预留三块，再分配剩余两块。令

y3=x3−3.y_3=x_3-3.y3​=x3​−3.

则 y3≥0y_3\ge0y3​≥0，方程变为

x1+x2+y3=2.x_1+x_2+y_3=2.x1​+x2​+y3​=2.

现在又是三个非负变量之和为二，隔板法给出

C(3+2−1,2)=C(4,2)=6.C(3+2-1,2)=C(4,2)=6.C(3+2−1,2)=C(4,2)=6.

这个变换可逆：每个原解减去第三变量的三，得到唯一新解；每个新解再加回三，得到唯一满足下限的原解。因此新旧解数相等。

六个原解为 (0,0,5),(1,0,4),(0,1,4),(2,0,3),(1,1,3),(0,2,3)(0,0,5),(1,0,4),(0,1,4),(2,0,3),(1,1,3),(0,2,3)(0,0,5),(1,0,4),(0,1,4),(2,0,3),(1,1,3),(0,2,3)，可作检查。

一般遇到 xi≥aix_i\ge a_ixi​≥ai​，令 yi=xi−aiy_i=x_i-a_iyi​=xi​−ai​，并从总和中减去所有下限。变量个数不变，只是可自由分配的总量变少；若总量小于下限之和，则无解。

有上限的整数解：减去超限情况

本页解答采用的条件是第三种饼干最多选两块，即

x1+x2+x3=5,xi≥0,x3≤2.x_1+x_2+x_3=5,\qquad x_i\ge0,\quad x_3\le2.x1​+x2​+x3​=5,xi​≥0,x3​≤2.

所有非负解共 C(7,5)=21C(7,5)=21C(7,5)=21。不符合上限的情况是 x3≥3x_3\ge3x3​≥3，不是 x3≥2x_3\ge2x3​≥2。

令 y3=x3−3≥0y_3=x_3-3\ge0y3​=x3​−3≥0，超限方程转为 x1+x2+y3=2x_1+x_2+y_3=2x1​+x2​+y3​=2，共有 C(4,2)=6C(4,2)=6C(4,2)=6 个解。因此合法数为

21−6=15.21-6=15.21−6=15.

也可按 x3=0,1,2x_3=0,1,2x3​=0,1,2 分类，剩余两变量的解数分别为 6,5,46,5,46,5,4，总计 15。

原页题干先说第 3 类库存只有两块，随后又说“kind 1 最多两块”，而解答始终使用 x3x_3x3​。若本意是仅有一个类别受上限约束，无论该类编号为 1 还是 3，对称性都给出 15。

若严格同时保留“第 3 类库存至多两块”和“第 1 类也至多两块”，则是两个限制，答案变成 9：x1,x3x_1,x_3x1​,x3​ 各取 0、1、2，九种搭配的和都不超过 5，x2=5−x1−x3x_2=5-x_1-x_3x2​=5−x1​−x3​ 唯一确定。不能将这一不同模型也解释为 15。

上限通常适合先数全部，再减去超限；多项上限还要处理超限情况的交叠。

In how many different ways can we write 100 as the sum of three non-negative integers? In other words, how many lists x1x2x3x_1x_2x_3x1​x2​x3​ of three non-negative integers x1x_1x1​, x2x_2x2​ and x3x_3x3​ are there such that x1+x2+x3=100x_1+x_2+x_3=100x1​+x2​+x3​=100?

You might need to use the Bijection principle for this question. Clearly state the two sets involved, and the function between the two sets.

查看答案与解析

Answer. (1022)=5151\binom{102}{2}=5151(2102​)=5151.

Derivation/proof. Map a triple to 100 stars separated into blocks of sizes x1,x2,x3x_1,x_2,x_3x1​,x2​,x3​ by two bars, allowing adjacent bars and end bars. This is a bijection with placements of two bars among 102 positions, hence (1022)\binom{102}{2}(2102​).

Explicitly, SSS is the set of ordered nonnegative triples of sum 100100100, and TTT is the set of length-102102102 strings with 100100100 identical stars and two identical bars. Counting stars in the three blocks inverts the map, proving bijectivity. Thus ∣S∣=∣T∣=(1022)=5151|S|=|T|=\binom{102}{2}=5151∣S∣=∣T∣=(2102​)=5151.

练习 2 · 2017 Midterm 10(b)

Q-2017-MID-P10-b · 原题 PDF · 第 12 页

Assume you have 50 candies and four boxes, labeled A,B,CA,B,CA,B,C, and DDD.

(b) How many ways can you put the candies in the boxes, assuming that the candies are identical?

查看答案与解析

Answer. (533)\binom{53}{3}(353​).

Derivation/proof. Let xA,xB,xC,xDx_A,x_B,x_C,x_DxA​,xB​,xC​,xD​ be nonnegative box counts, with sum 505050. Represent a distribution by fifty stars and three separators; the numbers of stars before and between separators specify the labeled boxes. Choosing the three separator positions among 53 gives (533)\binom{53}{3}(353​).

Write the occupancies as nonnegative integers satisfying xA+xB+xC+xD=50x_A+x_B+x_C+x_D=50xA​+xB​+xC​+xD​=50. A stars-and-bars arrangement and its occupancy vector determine each other, establishing a one-to-one correspondence even when some boxes are empty.

Q-2017-MID-P10-c · 原题 PDF · 第 12 页

Assume you have 50 candies and four boxes, labeled A,B,CA,B,CA,B,C, and DDD.

(c) How many ways can you put the candies in the boxes, assuming that the candies are identical and each box must have at least five candies put into it?

查看答案与解析

Answer. (333)=5456\binom{33}{3}=5456(333​)=5456.

Derivation/proof. Write each box count as xi=5+yix_i=5+y_ixi​=5+yi​, where yi≥0y_i\ge0yi​≥0. Then y1+y2+y3+y4=50−4⋅5=30y_1+y_2+y_3+y_4=50-4\cdot5=30y1​+y2​+y3​+y4​=50−4⋅5=30. Stars and bars gives (30+4−13)=(333)=5456\binom{30+4-1}{3}=\binom{33}{3}=5456(330+4−1​)=(333​)=5456. Independently, direct enumeration of nonnegative quadruples summing to 30 gives the same 5456; the bijection adds five to each coordinate.

For the independent enumeration, let y1=0,…,30y_1=0,\ldots,30y1​=0,…,30, y2=0,…,30−y1y_2=0,\ldots,30-y_1y2​=0,…,30−y1​, and y3=0,…,30−y1−y2y_3=0,\ldots,30-y_1-y_2y3​=0,…,30−y1​−y2​; y4y_4y4​ is determined by the sum. This counts 545654565456 quadruples.

The four required allocations reserve twenty balls in total, leaving 50−20=3050-20=3050−20=30 freely distributed balls. Apply stars and bars to those thirty, rather than to the printed thirty-five.
