Permutations
In how many ways can we select 
three
 students from a group of 
five
 students to stand in line for a photo?
Note:
 
the order in which the students are selected matters
 because it changes the order in the photo
Solution:
There are 5 ways to select the first student to stand at the start of the line. Afterwards, there are 4 ways to select the student to stand second in the line from the remaining 4 students. Finally, there are 3 ways to select the third student in line. By the 
product rule
, there are a total of 
 ways
 
2
