-Combinations
Example
In how many ways can we select 
three
 students to form a study-team, from a group of 
four
 students?
Note: 
the order in which the students are selected does not matter
! 
Solution:
We need only to find the number of subsets containing 3 elements from the set of 4 students. There are 4 such subsets (each subset excludes one student). Therefore, there are 4 ways to choose the three students for the team.

: How many bit strings of length 
four
 have exactly 
three
 1?
