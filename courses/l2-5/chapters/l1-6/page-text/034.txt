Permutations with 
Indistinguishable
 Objects
How many different strings can be made by reordering the letters of the word 
SUCCESS
?
Solution:
Since some of the letters repeat in SUCCESS, so the number of permutations for 7 distinct letters is not the correct answer here. SUCCESS contains 3 S’s, 2 C’s, 1 E and 1 U. The three 
indistinguishable
 S’s can be placed among the 7 positions in 
 different ways, leaving 4 positions free. Then, the two C’s can be placed in 
 ways, leaving 2 free positions. … By the product rule, the number of different ways is 
 
34
