Exercise
 on Ball Selection
3
 balls are selected randomly from a bag with 
36
 balls. If the bag contains 
8 yellow
 balls, 
7 green
 balls, 
6 blue
 balls, 
5 red
 balls, and 10 black balls. How many ways are there such that at least 
1 blue ball
 is selected? 
Solution
There are C(36,3) to select 3 balls with no constraints. 
There are C(30,3) ways to select 3 balls that are not blue. 
Thus, the number of ways to select 3 balls with at least one blue is: C(36,3)-C(30,3)=3080.
