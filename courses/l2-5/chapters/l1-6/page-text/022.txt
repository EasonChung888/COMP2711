Exercise
 on Earring Selection
Mary has 
9 different earrings
. Mary 
must wear at least one earring
. 
Each ear has two piercings
 for wearing an earring. It is not necessary for her to wear all the 4 earrings on her ears, e.g. she can wear one earring and leave the other 3 piercings empty. How many possible ways for Mary to wear her 9 earrings 
if the position of the earrings matters
? 
Solution
: 
If Mary wears 4 earrings there are 9x8x7x6 permutations. 
If Mary wears 3 earrings we must select the 3 piercings in C(4,3) ways, and permute the earrings in 9x8x7 ways. 
If Mary wears 2 earrings we must select the 2 piercings in C(4,2) ways, and permute the earrings in 9x8 ways. 
If Mary wears 1 earring we must select the 1 piercing in C(4,1) ways, and permute the earrings in 9 ways.
Sum of all ways is:  C(4,3)x9x8x7 + C(4,2)x9x8 + C(4,1)x9 = 5508
