Exercise
 on Group Selection
There are 
7
 boys and 
5
 girls in a class. We want to form a 
4
-person group from these 12 people, so that there is 
at least 1 girl
 in the group. In how many ways can the 4-person group be chosen?
Solution:
Select without any constraint C(12,4)=495.
Select only boys C(7,4)=35. 
Select at least one girl: C(12,4)- C(7,4)=460.
Alternative
 
Solution:
 The group may contain:
4 girls in C(5,4)=5 ways, or 
3 girls and 1 boy in C(5,3)
xC
(7,1)=10x7=70 ways, or
2 girls and 2 boys in C(5,2)
xC
(7,2)=10x210=210 ways, or
1 girl and 3 boys in C(5,1)
xC
(7,3) = 5x35 = 175 ways. 
The sum of all ways is 460.
