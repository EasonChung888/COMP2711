Assume that in any decimal number the leftmost digit cannot be 0. How many 6-digit numbers exist such that 
their digits from left to right
 (i.e., no two digits are identical) 
(a) 
Are strictly decreasing (e.g., 864320). 
Hint
: Any six different digits give you a unique number with strictly decreasing digits. 
By choosing any 6 digits from {0, 1, 2, · · · , 9} we can form a unique 6 digit number that its digits are strictly decreasing from left to right. The answer is C(10,6)
 
(b) 
Are strictly increasing (e.g., 136789).  
Similar to part (a), but we can not pick 0 because then it has to be the leftmost digit. So, the answer is C(9,6)
