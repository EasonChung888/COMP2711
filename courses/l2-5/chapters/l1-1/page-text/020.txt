Exercise: Knight and Knave Puzzle 3
  ▪  A says: “B is a knight” (q)
  ▪  B says: “The two of us are opposite types” (p⊕q)
  Question: What you can say about A and B (knight/knave)?
  p :“A is a knight”, q:“B is a knight”.

  1] Assume that A is a knight (p is true). Then q is true, and both A
  and B are knights. But this contradicts the statement of B that A
  and B are opposite types. Therefore, A cannot be a knight.
  2] Assume that A is a knave (p is false). Then, q is false. Since B is
  a knave, his statement is false. Therefore, both A and B are
  knaves.
