 Proposition


▪ Definition
    A  proposition       is  a   declarative     statement      (i.e.,  a
  sentence      that  declares     a  fact)  that  is  either   true   or
  false, but not both.
▪ Definition
    The truth value of a proposition is true, denoted by T,
  if it is a  true  proposition,     and false, denoted by        F, if it
  is a false proposition.
▪ Remark
    Propositions     are  the   basic   building    blocks    of   logic.
  The area of logic that deals with propositions is called
  propositional logic.
