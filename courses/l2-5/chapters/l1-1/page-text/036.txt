 De Morgan’s law examples

▪  ¬(p∨q) ≡ ¬p^¬q
▪  Example:   Express the negation of “Alice will send a secret message
   or Bob will send a secret message”
▪  𝑝: Alice will send a secret message
▪  𝑞: Bob will send a secret message
▪  By ¬(p∨q)   ≡  ¬p^¬q   : “Alice will not send a secret message        and   Bob
   will not send a secret message.”
▪  Another form of De Morgan law: ¬(p^q) ≡ ¬p∨¬q
▪  Example: Express the negation of “today          is Saturday and today      is a
   holiday”
▪  𝑝: today is Saturday
▪  𝑞: today is a holiday
▪  By ¬(p^q) ≡ ¬p∨¬q: “today is not Saturday or today is not a holiday”
