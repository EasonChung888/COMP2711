   Exercise: Logic Puzzle

▪  Determine the relative salaries of three coworkers using two facts.
1.   If Fred is not the highest paid of the three, then Janice is.
2.   If Janice is not the lowest paid, then Maggie is paid the most.
▪  Determine the relative salaries of Fred, Maggie, and Janice.
Notation:   (F=1)   denotes    the   proposition     that  Fred   is the   highest   paid,   (J=3)
   Janice is the lowest paid of the three etc.
1. I interpret the first fact as ¬(F=1) →       (J=1) ≡ (F=1)∨(J=1)
   Either (F=1) or     (J=1) is true  (but not both since only one person can              be the
   most highly paid)
2. I interpret the second fact as ¬(J=3) → (M=1) ≡ (J=3) ∨ (M=1).

Assume that (J=1). Then the second fact cannot be true because M≠1 and J≠3.
Therefore, it   must    be that (F=1). For the second         fact to be truth, it must hold
   that (J=3).
Thus, the (decreasing) order of salaries is Fred, Maggie and Janice.
