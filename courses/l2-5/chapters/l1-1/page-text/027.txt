 Ambiguity in natural language

▪ Sometimes, “if … then …” in natural language
  actually means “if and only if”.
▪ “If you finish your meal, then you can have
  dessert”
▪ What if you don’t finish your meal?
   ▪ In natural language: then you can’t have dessert.
   ▪ In logic: unspecified
▪ In this course, we should avoid such
  ambiguities.
