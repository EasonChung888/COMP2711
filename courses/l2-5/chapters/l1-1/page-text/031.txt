 Tautology and Contradiction

▪ A tautology is a compound proposition that is
  always true (e.g. p∨¬p).
▪ A  contradiction      is  a  compound      proposition
  that is always false (e.g. p^¬p).
▪ A contingency is a compound proposition that
  is neither a tautology nor a contradiction.
