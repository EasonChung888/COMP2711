Propositional Equivalence Example 2
Show that (p^q)→(p∨q) is a tautology  by developing a
series of logical equivalences:

(p^q)→(p∨q) ≡ ¬ (p^q) ∨ (p∨q) (Equiv. def. of implication)
                 ≡ (¬p∨¬q) ∨ (p∨q) (DeMorgan)
                 ≡ (¬p∨p) ∨ (¬q∨q) (Associative)
                 ≡ T ∨ T ≡ T
Note: You do not need to remember the names of the
