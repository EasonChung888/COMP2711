 Examples

▪ These statements are not propositions:
   (a) No parking
   (b) Who has an iPad?
   (c) y = log(x+1)
   (d) x2 – 3x + 1 = 0

  (c) and (d) can become propositions if x and y are
  assigned values.
▪ Logical paradoxes are not propositions
   (a) This sentence is false (Liar paradox)
   (b) All I know is that I know nothing (Socratic paradox)
