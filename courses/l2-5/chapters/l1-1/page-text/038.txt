  Exercise on Tautologies

▪ Is p ∨ (p→q) a tautology?
▪ p∨(p→q) ≡ p∨(¬p ∨ q) ≡ (p ∨¬p) ∨ q ≡ T ∨ q ≡ T
▪ Tautology

▪ Is p ∨ (q→p) a tautology?
▪  p ∨ (q→p) ≡ p ∨ (¬q ∨ p) ≡ (p ∨ p) ∨ ¬q ≡ p ∨ ¬q
▪ Not Tautology (when p is F and q is T, p ∨ (q→p) is F).
