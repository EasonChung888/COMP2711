 Converse, Inverse, Contrapositive

“If I am elected (p), then taxes will be lowered (q).”
▪ The contrapositive of p→q is ¬q→¬p.
   ▪  if taxes are not lowered, then I am not elected
▪ The converse of p→q is q→p.
   ▪  if taxes are lowered, then I am elected
▪ The inverse of p→q is ¬p → ¬q.
   ▪  if I am not elected, then taxes will not be lowered
▪ p→q and its contrapositive are equivalent.
