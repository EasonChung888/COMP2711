 The use of ≡,              , =

▪ ≡   (⇔)   denotes     logical   equivalence      regardless
  of truth values of the propositional variables.
▪    is a binary logical operator.
▪ =  is  not  used    on  propositions     . It is  only   used
  between two mathematical objects.
▪  Examples:
   ▪ (𝑝 → 𝑞)    (¬𝑝 ∨ 𝑞) ≡ 𝑇
   ▪ (𝑝    𝑞) ≡ (𝑝 → 𝑞 ∧ 𝑞 → 𝑝)
   ▪ (𝑇    𝐹) ≡ 𝐹
   ▪ (2 = 3) ∧ (5 ≠ 4) ≡ 𝐹
   ▪ 𝑝: 2 + 2 = 4, 𝑝 is true
