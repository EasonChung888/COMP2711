      Many interesting problems  (Logic)

▪ Knights and Knaves
   ▪ There are two kinds of inhabitants
     on an island, knights, who always
     tell the truth, and knaves, who
     always lie.
   ▪ You encounter two people A and B.
   ▪ A says: “B is a knight”
   ▪ B says: “A is a knight”

   ▪ Question: What you can say about A and B
     (knight/knave)?
