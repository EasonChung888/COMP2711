 Examples

▪ “If Beijing is the capital of China, then Beijing is a city
  of China”.
▪ True.
▪ “If Beijing is a city of China, then Beijing is the capital
  of China.”
▪ True.
▪ “If the moon is made of green cheese, then I have
  more money than Bill Gates. ”
▪ True.
▪ “If the moon is not made of green cheese, then I have
  more money than Bill Gates. ”
▪ False.
