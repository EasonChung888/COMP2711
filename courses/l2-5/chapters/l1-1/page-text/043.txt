Propositional Equivalence Example 1
Show that ¬(p∨(¬p^q)) and ¬p^¬q are logically
equivalent by developing a series of logical
equivalences:

¬(p∨(¬p^q))       ≡ ¬p ^ (¬(¬p ^ q)) (De Morgan)
                  ≡ ¬p ^ (¬(¬p) ∨ ¬q) (De Morgan)
                  ≡ ¬p ^ (p ∨ ¬q) (Double Negation)
                  ≡ (¬p ^ p) ∨ (¬p ^ ¬q) (Distributive)
                  ≡ F ∨ (¬p ^ ¬q) (Negation)
                  ≡ ¬p ^ ¬q (Identity)
