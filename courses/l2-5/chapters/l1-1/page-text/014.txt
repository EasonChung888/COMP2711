 Negation

▪ Definition
  Let p be a proposition. The negation of p, denoted by
  ¬p or 𝑝ҧ and read as “NOT p”, is the statement “it is not
  the case that p”. The truth value of ¬p is the opposite
  of the truth value of p.




▪ Example:
   ▪  p = “Tom’s iPhone has at least 32GB of memory”
   ▪  ¬p  =  “It is not  the   case  that  Tom’s    iPhone    has  at
      least 32GB of memory”
   ▪  ¬p = “Tom’s iPhone has less than 32GB of memory”14
