 Conditional Statement
▪ Definition
  The conditional statement p→q is the proposition “if
  p, then q”. Also called an implication (p implies q).
▪ p is called the hypothesis (or premise) and q is called
  the conclusion (or consequence).







▪ Note: There does not need to be any connection
  between the hypothesis and the conclusion.
  The true value of p →q depends only on the truth
  values of p and q.                                             21
