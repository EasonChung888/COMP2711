      Monty Hall Problem (Probability)

▪ Suppose you're on a game show, and you're given
  the choice of three doors: Behind one door is a
  prize; behind the others, goats. You pick a door,
  say No.    1. The host, who knows what's behind
  the doors, opens another door, say No.             2, which
  has a goat. He then asks to you, “Do you want to
  switch to door No.       3?”



     Answer 1: It’s 50-50, no need
     to switch.
     Answer 2: Switch to door No 3.
     87% respondents gave the wrong
     answer in a survey
