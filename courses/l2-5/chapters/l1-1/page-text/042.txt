 Notes on Propositional Equivalences

▪ All laws (equivalences) van be proven using
  truth tables
▪ Sometimes you do not need the full truth table
   ▪ Example of absorption law: p∨(p^q) ≡ p
   ▪ When p is T(F), p∨(p^q) is also T(F), irrespectively of
     the truth value of q
▪ Sometimes instead of truth tables, you can use
  other laws:
   ▪ Example for implication law: (p→q)^(p→r)≡p→(q^r)
   ▪ (p→q)^(p→r) ≡ (¬p∨q)^(¬p∨r) ≡
   ▪ ¬p∨(q^r) (distributive)  ≡ p→(q^r)
