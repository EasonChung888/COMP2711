         What is discrete mathematics?

▪ Part of mathematics devoted to the study of discrete
  objects.
   ▪  E.g., counting objects/events, analyzing processes
      (algorithms) with a finite set of steps.
▪ Topics in discrete mathematics
   ▪  Logic
   ▪  Counting (combinatorics)
   ▪  Sets and their cardinality
   ▪  (Discrete) probability theory
   ▪  Number theory and cryptography
   ▪  Induction and Recurrence
   ▪  Algorithms and Asymptotic Notation
   ▪  Graph theory
