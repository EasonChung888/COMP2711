Different Knights and Knaves Variant

You are on the island of knights and knaves and you want to find the way
to the town. At a fork on the road, you have to decide whether to go left or
right. There is a knight and a knave, but you do not know who is who. You
chose one at random to ask for directions.
▪  What question should you ask, in order to find the correct way to the
   town.
Example: If you ask "Which is the direction to the town", you will get the
correct direction from the knight and the wrong direction from the knave.
You should ask a different question.
▪  Solution: "What the other person would answer if I asked which is the
   direction to the town"
▪  Explanation:
The knight would give the wrong direction, because this is what the               knave
would say.
The knave would also give the wrong direction, because he would reverse
the knight's answer.
Thus, you should always go to the opposite direction of the answer.
