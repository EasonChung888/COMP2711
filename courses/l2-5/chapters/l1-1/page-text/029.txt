2: You are shown an open envelope – do you need to turn it to check
whether it contains a stamp?
No. The rule does not apply to open envelopes. They may contain a
stamp or not. Since I have ¬p, p→q is T, and I do not care about q.
3: You are shown an envelope with a stamp – do you need to turn it
to check whether it is sealed?
No. I do not care whether the envelope is sealed or open. Since I have
q, I do not care about whether it is the case of p or ¬p.
4: You are shown an envelope without a stamp – do you need to turn
it to check whether it is sealed?
Yes. The envelope must be open, otherwise there is a rule violation.
Since I have ¬q, it must be ¬p (contrapositive: ¬q→¬p).
The majority of human subjects gave the wrong answer for case                4.
