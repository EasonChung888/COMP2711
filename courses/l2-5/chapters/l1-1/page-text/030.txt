 Precedence of Logical Operators

▪ Multiple logical operators
  can be used to construct
  compound propositions.
▪ Parentheses can be used for
  clarity
            p ∨ ¬q →  p ^ q,
   may be written more clearly as
        (p ∨ ¬q) →    (p ^ q).
▪ Remark
   ▪  Boolean operations in programming languages (e.g., ||, ^, !, ==
      in C) correspond to logical operators
   ▪  “if … then …” statements in programming languages do         not
      correspond to   →
