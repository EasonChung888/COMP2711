 Modular Arithmetic


 Example
  Prove the property
              (𝑎 mod 𝑚𝑛)  mod  𝑛 = 𝑎 mod  𝑛
 Proof:
    𝑎 = 𝑞𝑚𝑛  + 𝑠,0 ≤ 𝑠 < 𝑚𝑛
    𝑠=  𝑝𝑛 + 𝑟,0≤  𝑟<  𝑛
    Then, (𝑎 mod  𝑚𝑛) mod  𝑛 = 𝑟
    On the other hand
                   𝑎 = (𝑞𝑚  + 𝑝)𝑛 + 𝑟,
    So, 𝑎mod  𝑛 =  𝑟
    The equation is proved.
