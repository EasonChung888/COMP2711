  Modular Arithmetic and Congruences

 Difference:
    +𝑚  and ⋅𝑚  are defined only on elements of 𝐙𝑚
    Congruences are defined over 𝐙
 Examples
    6 +8 7 = 5
    6 + 15 ≡ 5 (mod  8)
    6 + 15 ≡ 21  (mod 8)
    Can’t write 6 +8 7 =  13 or 6+8  15 = 5
