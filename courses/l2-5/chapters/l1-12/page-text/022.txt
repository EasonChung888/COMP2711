 More on Congruences

 Theorem
  Let 𝑚  be a positive integer. If 𝑎 ≡  𝑏 (mod 𝑚)  and 𝑐  ≡
  𝑑 (mod  𝑚), then
                   𝑎 + 𝑐 ≡ 𝑏 + 𝑑 (mod  𝑚)
                   𝑎𝑐 ≡ 𝑏𝑑  mod  𝑚
  Proof in textbook
 Example
  Because 7   ≡ 2 (mod   5) and  11 ≡  1 (mod  5) , it follows
  that
       18 = 7 + 11  ≡  2 + 1 = 3 (mod  5)
       77 = 7 ⋅11  ≡  2 ⋅1 = 2 (mod   5)
