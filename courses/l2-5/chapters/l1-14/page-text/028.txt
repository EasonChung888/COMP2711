 Fermat Little Theorem

▪ Lemma:     Let 𝑝  be a prime number. For any non-zero
  𝑎 ∈ 𝐙𝑝, 1 ∙𝑝 𝑎,  2 ∙𝑝𝑎,  ,    (𝑝 − 1) ∙𝑝 𝑎
   are a permutation of the set     {1,2,,  𝑝 − 1}
▪ Proof sketch
   ▪ None of them is 0
   ▪ If 𝑥 ⋅𝑎 ≡ 𝑦 ⋅𝑎  mod   𝑝 ,then 𝑥  ⋅𝑎 ⋅𝑎−1  ≡ 𝑦 ⋅𝑎 ⋅ 𝑎−1,𝑥 ≡  𝑦
