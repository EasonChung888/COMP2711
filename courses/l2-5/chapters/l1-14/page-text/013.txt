 A Hard Problem: Discrete Logarithm

▪ Discrete logarithm is one such problem
   ▪ Inverse of modular exponentiation
   ▪ Given a prime 𝑝     (potentially very large) and 𝑟,   𝑎  ∈ 𝐙𝑝,
     find 𝑥  such that
                       𝑎𝑥 ≡ 𝑟 (mod  𝑝)
▪ Modular exponential and discrete logarithm is
  believed   to be a one-way function
   ▪ Yes, if you can solve discrete logarithm, you can
     break current crypto systems
▪ In 2015, it was reported that for 512-bit primes, the
  problem can be solved with a few thousands of CPUs
  in a week
   ▪ Estimated cost to break 1024 bits: US$100 million.
