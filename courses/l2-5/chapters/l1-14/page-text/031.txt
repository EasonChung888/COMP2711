 Fermat’s Little Theorem


                                                     Pierre de Fermat
▪ Corollary                                          (1601-1665)
  If 𝑝 is prime and 𝑎    is an integer not divisible by 𝑝, then
                       𝑎𝑝−1  ≡ 1  mod   𝑝
▪ Examples:
       96 ≡ 26  ≡ 1  mod  7           146 ≡ 0  mod   7
▪ Useful in computing the remainders of large powers
▪ Example:
  Find  7222  mod  11.
  By the theorem, we know that 710         ≡ 1 (mod  11), and so
  (710 )𝑘 ≡ 1 (mod  11), for any positive integer 𝑘.
  Therefore,
   ▪ 7222 = 722⋅10+2 =   710  22⋅ 72 ≡ 122 ⋅ 49 ≡ 5 (mod   11)
