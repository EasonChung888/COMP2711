 Monty Hall Problem

 Behind one door is a prize; behind the others, goats.
 You pick a door, say door 1
 The host looks behind the 3 doors.
     If your pick is incorrect, the host opens the other
      door with a goat
     If your pick is correct, the host randomly chooses
      another door and open it
 Now he asks: Do you want to change your mind?
 Answer:    Change!
 Explanation 1:     Your first pick is correct with
  probability 1/3. This doesn’t change after the host
  opens a door. The 3rd      door then must have probability
  2/3 to have the prize.
