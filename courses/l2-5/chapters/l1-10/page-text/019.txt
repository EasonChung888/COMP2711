 Example

 The market share of a particular device is as follows:
     Company A: 80%
     Company B: 15%
     Company C: 5%
 The defect rate of devices by these companies:
     Company A: 4%
     Company B: 6%
     Company C: 9%
 Questions
     Given a random device from the market, what are the
      probabilities that it is made by these 3 companies,
      respectively?
     How do the results change if the random device is found
      to be defective?
