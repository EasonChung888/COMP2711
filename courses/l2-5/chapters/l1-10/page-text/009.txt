 Bayes’ Theorem: Example

 Breathalyzers have an error rate of 5%.
     A sober driver is detected as “drunk” with prob. 5%
     A drunk driver is not detected with prob. 5%
 1 in 1000 drivers is driving drunk
 Police officers stops a driver at random
 Breathalyzer detects “drunk”
 Question
  What’s the probability that the driver is really drunk?
 Answer
  95%?  Wrong! 𝑝(drunk | detected) ≠         𝑝(detected | drunk)
 Answer
  2%
