 Counterexample: we cannot distribute a
 existential quantifier over a conjunction

∃x (P(x) ^  Q(x))        ∃x P(x) ^ ∃x Q(x)
Assume that the domain of x is the set of integers and
  the predicates     P(x): 2x + 1 = 5     Q(x): x2 = 9
▪ ∃x P(x) ^ ∃x Q(x) (equivalent: ∃x P(x) ^ ∃y Q(y))
   ▪   is true because P(2) and Q(3) are true.
▪ ∃x (P(x) ^   Q(x))
   ▪   is false because there is no one integer a such that P(a) and
      Q(a) are both true.
▪ Therefore: ∃x P(x) ^ ∃x Q(x) is NOT equivalent to
  ∃x (P(x) ^   Q(x))
