 Negation Example


▪ Show that ¬∀x (P(x)→Q(x)) and ∃x (P(x) ^ ¬Q(x))
   are logically equivalent.

▪ Solution
         ¬∀x (P(x)→Q(x)) ≡    ∃x ¬ ((P(x)  →Q(x))
                            ≡ ∃x ¬ (¬ P(x)   ∨ Q(x))
                            ≡ ∃x (P(x)  ^ ¬Q(x))
