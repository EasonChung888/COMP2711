  PNF: Prenex Normal Form

▪  PNF means that all the quantifiers are in the beginning
▪  Every formula in predicate logic can be written in PNF
▪  When I convert to PNF, I can use Rules 1-4, provided that all
   quantifiers are not negated, and all operators are ∨                or ∧.
▪  Conversion Steps
1.  Use different variable names
    ▪  ∃x P(x) → ∀x Q(x) should change to     ∃x P(x) → ∀y Q(y)
2.  If there are operators other than ∨           or ∧, replace them with
    equivalent expressions that contain only ∨              or ∧
    ▪  ∃x P(x) → ∀y Q(y) should change to ¬∃x P(x) ∨ ∀y Q(y)
3.  Make sure that none of the quantifiers is negated
    ▪   ¬∃x P(x) ∨ ∀y Q(y) should change to   ∀x ¬P(x)  ∨ ∀y Q(y)
▪  Now you are ready to apply Rules 1-4
    ▪  If you extend the scope of   ∀x, and then ∀y  you get ∀x∀y (¬P(x) ∨ Q(y))
    ▪  If you extend the scope of   ∀y, and then ∀x  you get ∀y∀x (¬P(x) ∨ Q(y))
▪  ∃xP(x)→∀yQ(y) ≡ ∀x∀y(¬P(x)∨Q(y)) ≡ ∀x∀y(P(x)→Q(y))                    ≡
