 Example on Restricted Domains 2
▪  Let  P(x), Q(x),  and  R(x)  be  the  statements    “x  is a professor”,   “x  is
   ignorant” , and “x is vain”, respectively.
   Assume that the domain consists of all people
▪  Express the statements using quantifiers and P(x), Q(x), and R(x).
▪   All professors are not ignorant.
         ∀x (P(x) →   ¬Q(x))
▪   All ignorant people are vain.
         ∀x (Q(x) →   R(x))
▪   All professors are not vain.
         ∀x (P(x) →   ¬R(x))
▪   Does the last conclusion follow the first two statements?
         No. There may well be vain professors, since the premises do
not  rule  out  the  possibility   that  there   are  vain  people    besides   the
ignorant ones.
