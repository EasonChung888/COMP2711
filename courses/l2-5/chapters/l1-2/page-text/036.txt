  Math Examples

▪  Translate the statement “the sum of two positive
   integers is always positive” into a logical expression.
    ▪ Assuming that the domain is positive integers:
    ▪ ∀x ∀y (x+y>0)
    ▪ Assuming that the domain is all integers:
    ▪ ∀x ∀y ((x>0 ^ y>0)    →  (x+y>0))

▪  Translate the statement “every nonzero real number
   has a multiplicative inverse”.
    ▪ ∀x ((x≠0)  →  ∃y (x•y =1)) ≡ ∀x   ∃y ((x≠0)  →  (x•y =1))
    ▪ Note: This not always straightforward to move the quantifiers
      in the beginning. See discussion about PNF later
