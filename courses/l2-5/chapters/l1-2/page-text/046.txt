  Natural Language Example and
  Negation

▪  Use quantifiers to express the statement “there is a woman who has
   taken a flight on every airline in the world”.
▪  Let P(w,f) be “woman w has taken flight f ”
▪  Let Q(f,a) be “f is a flight on airline a ”
   Solution:       ∃w ∀a ∃f ( (P(w, f) ^ Q(f, a) )
▪  Negation
    Use quantifiers to express       the  statement “there     does   not  exist  a  woman
   who has taken a flight on every airline in the world” so that no negation
   precedes a quantifier.
   ¬  ∃w   ∀a  ∃f  ((P(w,f)^Q(f,a))   ≡   ∀w  ¬∀a    ∃f ((P(w,f)^Q(f,a))    ≡  ∀w   ∃a  ¬  ∃f
   ((P(w,f)^Q(f,a))≡ ∀w ∃a∀f (¬(P(w,f)^Q(f,a)) ≡
   ∀w ∃a ∀f (¬P(w,f)∨¬Q(f,a)) ≡       ∀w ∃a ∀f (P(w,f)→¬Q(f,a))
   For every woman, there is an airline such that for all flights, this woman
   has   not  taken    that  flight  or  the  flight  does   not   belong    to  the  airline
   (alternatively,   if  the  woman     took   the   flight,  then   the  flight  does   not
   belong to the airline).
