  Order of Quantifiers Matters

▪   ∀x∃y Loves(x,y) means (domain is all persons)
    ▪ "For every person x, there is someone y that they love"
      (everyone loves somebody)
▪  ∃y∀x Loves(x,y) means
    ▪ "There is a single person y whom everyone loves"
▪   ∃x∀y Loves(x,y) means
    ▪ "There is a person x who loves everyone".
▪  ∀y∃x Loves(x,y) means
    ▪ "For every person y, there is someone who loves them"
      (everyone is loved by somebody)
▪  We can switch the order of consecutive identical
   quantifiers: ∀x∀y Loves(x,y) ≡ ∀y∀x Loves(x,y) .
