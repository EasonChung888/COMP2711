  Exercise on Equivalence

▪  Let P(x), and Q(x) be the statements “x is a professor”, “x is
   ignorant”.
▪  "All professors are not ignorant" can be expressed as ∀x (P(x) →
   ¬Q(x)).
▪  Which of the following statements is equivalent to ∀x (P(x) → ¬Q(x)):
        1. ∀x (¬ P(x) ∨ ¬Q(x))
        2. ¬∃x (P(x) ^ Q(x))
▪  Answer    1:Only 1
▪  Answer    2:Only 2
▪  Answer    3:Both 1. and 2.
▪  Correct answer: Answer       3:Both 1. and 2.
▪  ∀x (P(x) → ¬Q(x)) ≡ ∀x (¬ P(x) ∨ ¬Q(x)) ≡ ∀x ¬(P(x) ^ Q(x)) ≡ ¬∃x
   (P(x) ^ Q(x))
