 De Morgan's Laws for Quantifiers
 (cont'd)

▪ When the domain has n elements x1, x2, … , xn, it
  follows that
     ¬∀x P(x) is the same as ¬ (     P(x1) ^ P(x2) ^ … ^    P(xn)),
     which is equivalent to ¬    P(x1) ∨ ¬P(x2) ∨ … ∨ ¬P(xn) by
       De Morgan's laws. This is the same as            ∃x ¬P(x).

▪ Similarly, ¬∃x P(x) is the same as ¬ (P(x1) ∨ P(x2) ∨ … ∨
  P(xn)), which by De Morgan's laws is equivalent to
  ¬P(x1) ^ ¬   P(x2) ^ … ^ ¬   P(xn). This  is the same as     ∀x
  ¬P(x).
