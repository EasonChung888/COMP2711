  Negation Examples

▪ Example 1
   What is the negation of the statement
   ∀x (x2 > x) ?
▪ Let P(x) be: x2 > x
▪ ¬∀x P(x)   ≡  ∃x ¬P(x) ≡   ∃x (x2 ≤ x)
▪ Example 2
   What is the negation of the statement
   ∃x (x2 = 2) ?
▪ Let P(x) be: x2 = 2
▪ ¬∃x P(x) ≡   ∀x ¬P(x)   ≡  ∀x (x2 ≠ 2)
