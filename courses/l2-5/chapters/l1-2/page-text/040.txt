 Negating Nested Quantifiers


▪ Rule
To negate a statement with a series of quantifiers “flip”
the quantifiers and negate the remaining statement
▪ Example
Express the negation of the statement ∀x∃y (xy = 1) so
  that no negation precedes a quantifier.
¬∀x ∃y (xy = 1) ≡
∃x ¬∃y (xy = 1) ≡
∃x ∀y ¬(xy = 1) ≡
∃x ∀y (xy ≠ 1)
