 Counterexample: we cannot distribute a
 universal quantifier over a disjunction

▪ Show with a counter example that ∀x (P(x) ∨            Q(x)) is
  not equivalent to ∀x P(x) ∨ ∀x Q(x).
   ▪  We already saw a counter example on Slide 14
   ▪  Note: recall that ∀x P(x) ∨ ∀x Q(x) ≡∀x P(x) ∨ ∀y Q(y)
▪ Let P(x) the predicate "x is male"
▪ Let Q(y) the predicate "y is female"
▪ Let the domain be all human.
▪ ∀x (P(x) ∨   Q(x)) means that each human is male or
  female (true).
▪ ∀x P(x) ∨ ∀y Q(y) means all humans are male or all
  humans are female (false)
