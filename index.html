Logic, one move at a time.

Fourteen short lessons turn the three COMP2711 Logic lectures into a guided path: understand the notation, watch each move, try a small check, then build toward a 20-point mini-mock.

Your finish line

Score at least 14/20 on the final unseen mini-mock, while being able to explain why your translations, equivalence steps, inference rules, and proof method are valid.

How to use this course

  1. Open one lesson at a time. Ordinary lessons take about 12-18 minutes.
  2. Read the plain-English intuition before the formal definition.
  3. Attempt each check before opening its hint. Browser feedback is saved locally.
  4. For a text box, copy your response and paste it into the chat. Your teacher will mark the first incorrect step and adapt the next lesson.
  5. Reach 7/10 on Day 1 before starting Day 2. Reach 14/20 on the final mini-mock to complete the mission.
