# Teaching Notes

- Learner profile: complete beginner in Logic.
- Language: all instructional and assessment content in English; coordination may be in Chinese.
- Pace: two days, two to three hours per day.
- Style: explanation-first. Use plain-English intuition before formal notation, then annotate every worked step.
- Lesson size: short sequential pages, not long lecture notes.
- Interaction: two short checks inside ordinary lessons; larger practice blocks only at the Day 1 checkpoint, inference lessons, and final mini-mock.
- Feedback: free responses are submitted in chat for line-by-line review. Identify the first invalid step before showing a repair.
- Mastery evidence: do not create a learning record merely because a page was viewed. Wait for a submitted answer or assessment result.
- Visual companion: declined during design. Local lesson HTML remains the delivery format.
