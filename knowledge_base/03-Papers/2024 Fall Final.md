## Source defects and page inspection

All 31 pages were inspected. Low-text pages 11,14,16,18,20,22,25,28 are blank numbered answer-space pages; 29–31 say Draft Paper. Problem 1(a) calls duplication of a disjunct a distributive law, although the equality itself is valid. Problem 3(a) says k−2 interior candidates in its answer prose but correctly prints C(k−1,2); its sampling distribution is unspecified. Problems 3(b),3(c)(i,ii),4(b)(i–iii) omit a sampling or independence condition required for the supplied probabilities. These remain audit-only. Problem 7 supplies only coefficient removal, so printed step 2 CRT completion is derived. Problem 9(a) supplies four mismatched classifications for five items; all five are independently derived with the printed labels preserved. Problem 10 remains audit-only: integer k includes negative values but the recurrence only determines n≥1; T(1/2) is unconstrained. Its corrected nonnegative-k formula and printed summation-denominator defect are retained in the audit without adding a domain assumption.

Problem 4(a) is also needs-review: arbitrary random variables need not have a defined variance. A symmetric discrete heavy-tailed counterexample makes the mean and variance undefined, so the finite-moment restriction needed by the page 13 proof is not inserted.
