# STEM-2025F-FINAL-P10 - Shared Problem Prompt

## Shared printed wording

A spider starts with 10 legs. Each day, its legs instantly double by splitting, but immediately after, exactly 2 legs fall off and are lost forever. Let $a_n$ be the number of legs after $n$ days, with $a_0=10$.
