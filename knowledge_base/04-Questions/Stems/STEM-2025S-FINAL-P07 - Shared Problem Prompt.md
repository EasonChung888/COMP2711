# STEM-2025S-FINAL-P07 - Shared Problem Prompt

## Shared printed wording

A drawer in a dark room contains 100 red socks, 80 green socks, 60 blue socks and 40 black socks. A youngster selects socks one at a time from the drawer but is unable to see the color of the socks drawn. (A pair of socks is two socks of the same color. No sock may be counted in more than one pair.) Suppose your answer is $n$.
