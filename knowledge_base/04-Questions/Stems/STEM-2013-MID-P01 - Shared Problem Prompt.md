# STEM-2013-MID-P01 - Shared Problem Prompt

## Shared printed wording

Twelve Easter eggs are numbered $1,2,\ldots,12$. Eggs 1 to 4 are colored red, eggs 5 to 8 are colored green, and eggs 9 to 12 are colored purple. A child is allowed to choose 5 of the eggs.
