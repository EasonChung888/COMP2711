# STEM-2025S-FINAL-P04 - Shared Problem Prompt

## Shared printed wording

The least common multiple (LCM) of two integers $x$ and $y$ is the smallest positive integer that is a multiple of both $x$ and $y$. As an example, the least common multiple of 8 and 10 is 40.
