# STEM-2023F-FINAL-P04 - Shared Problem Prompt

## Shared printed wording

The CSE Department of HKUST has a total of $n$ light bulbs and $m$ switches. We know that each light bulb is connected to at least one switch. By flipping a switch, the status of every light bulb connected to it will change (so if it was OFF it will be turned ON and vice versa). Assume $X_i$ is the random variable that shows the status of the $i$th light bulb. $X_i=0$ means light bulb $i$ is OFF and $X_i=1$ means it is ON. Also, let $Y$ be the random variable that shows the number of light bulbs that are ON in the department. Note that the initial status of every light bulb is arbitrary.
