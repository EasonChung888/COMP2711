# STEM-2016-MID-P02 - Shared Problem Prompt

## Shared printed wording

Consider a university with 300 faculties, 500 teaching assistants, 600 postgraduate students and 10,000 undergraduate students. A tennis club would like to invite a group of guests to join an activity. The group of guests must consist of 4 faculties, 10 teaching assistants, 10 postgraduate students and 40 undergraduate students. Assume that each person initially has only one role (i.e. no one person can be both a postgraduate student and a teaching assistant, etc).
