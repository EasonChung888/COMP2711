## Question

![[STEM-2025S-MID-P04 - Shared Problem Prompt#Shared printed wording]]

(iv) The set of all polynomials with real coefficients and the degree is at most 5.

## Official Answer

Source: [[knowledge_base/past_paper/COMP2711_S2025_Midterm.pdf#page=6|physical PDF page 6]].

(iv) Uncountable.

## Supplementary Explanation

**Answer.** Uncountable.

**Derivation/proof.** The set still contains every constant polynomial. The map $t\mapsto(t,0,0,0,0,0)$ injects the real numbers into its six coefficient positions. If these tuples could be enumerated, projecting onto the first coefficient would enumerate every real number, a contradiction. Thus finitely many real coefficient positions already give an uncountable collection; the degree bound does not make it finite or countably infinite.
