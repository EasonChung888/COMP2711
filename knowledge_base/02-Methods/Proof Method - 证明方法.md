# Proof Method - 证明方法

## Applicability｜适用条件

Use for direct implication, contraposition, or contradiction proofs over integers and divisibility. 适用于结论为蕴含、析取或不可能性的命题。

## Procedure｜步骤

1. Choose a route matching the negated conclusion.
2. State assumptions precisely and expand them algebraically.
3. Derive the contradiction or conclusion, explicitly naming the theorem used.

## Failure Modes｜常见失误

Do not assume the statement being proved, and do not end with an unnamed ‘obvious contradiction’. 任何矛盾都要指出与哪项假设冲突。

## Inference chains and algebraic identities｜推理链与代数恒等式

For a chain of logical premises, list each statement with its premise or named rule: universal instantiation, modus ponens, modus tollens, simplification, or generalization. An arbitrary object may be generalized only after all assumptions specific to it have been discharged. 推理规则证明逐行注明依据，全称推广不能从特殊见证直接推出。

For an algebraic identity, start from one side, expand definitions such as binomial coefficients, and perform reversible operations with valid denominators until reaching the other side. 代数证明要检查除数非零和阶乘定义域，不能用仅仅复述等式代替推导。
