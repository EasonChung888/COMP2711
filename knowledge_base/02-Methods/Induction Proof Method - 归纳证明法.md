# Induction Proof Method - 归纳证明法

## Applicability｜适用条件

Use when the claim is indexed by non-negative or positive integers and the next case can be derived from established smaller cases. 适用于指数整除和递归定义的存在性。

## Procedure｜步骤

1. Prove every required base case.
2. State weak or strong induction hypothesis with its exact range.
3. Rewrite the next case until the hypothesis applies, then finish the algebra or construction.

## Failure Modes｜常见失误

Do not use an unproved $n$-case hypothesis and do not leave a gap between base cases and the induction step. 强归纳的范围必须包含所需的子问题。
