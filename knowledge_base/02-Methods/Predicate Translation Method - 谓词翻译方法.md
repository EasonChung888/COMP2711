# Predicate Translation Method - 谓词翻译方法

## Applicability｜适用条件

Use for English statements about all/some objects and for quantified equivalence tests. 适用于存在量词、全称量词和谓词关系。

## Procedure｜步骤

1. Declare the universe and each predicate.
2. Put restrictions in the antecedent of a universal implication; keep properties of one witness in one existential conjunction.
3. Move negations across quantifiers only by the correct dual law.

## Failure Modes｜常见失误

Do not allow different existential witnesses for linked properties, and do not drop a domain restriction. 同一人必须满足同一句中的所有合取条件。
