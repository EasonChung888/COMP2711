# Random-Variable Method - 随机变量方法

## Applicability｜适用条件

Use for expected waiting time, variance, or expected numbers of people/items satisfying a property. 适用于随机过程的平均次数和波动。

## Procedure｜步骤

1. Identify the distribution or define indicator variables.
2. Compute $E(X)$ and, when needed, $E(X^2)-E(X)^2$.
3. Use linearity for sums; add variances only after checking independence.

## Failure Modes｜常见失误

Do not confuse a geometric waiting time with a uniform location, and do not infer certainty from an expected waiting time. 期望不是保证发生的截止时间。
