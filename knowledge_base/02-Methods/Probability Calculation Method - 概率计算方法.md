# Probability Calculation Method - 概率计算方法

## Applicability｜适用条件

Use for equally likely finite outcomes, independent repeated trials, complements, and inclusion–exclusion events. 适用于先计数再除以样本空间，或处理独立重复试验。

## Procedure｜步骤

1. Define the sample space and named events.
2. Use a complement for ‘at least one’ and multiply only independent per-trial probabilities.
3. For overlapping missing-event conditions, apply inclusion–exclusion with the correct intersection count.

## Failure Modes｜常见失误

Do not multiply dependent event probabilities and do not subtract a union without correcting overlaps. 切勿在事件重叠时只做简单相减。
