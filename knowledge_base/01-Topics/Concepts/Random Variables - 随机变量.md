# Random Variables - 随机变量

## Definition｜定义

A random variable maps outcomes to numbers; $E(X)=\sum_xxP(X=x)$ and $V(X)=E(X^2)-E(X)^2$. 随机变量把随机过程的结果数值化，期望和方差描述中心与波动。

## Recognition Cues｜识别线索

Waiting time suggests a geometric distribution; a uniformly hidden location gives a discrete uniform distribution. ‘Expected number’ often benefits from indicator variables. 识别抽样是否重置。

## Worked-use note｜使用提醒

Linearity of expectation needs no independence, but adding variances does require independence or covariance control.
