# Probability Events - 概率事件

## Definition｜定义

An event is a subset of an explicitly chosen sample space; $P(A\mid B)=P(A\cap B)/P(B)$ when $P(B)>0$. 概率计算先明确样本空间及事件，条件概率再把范围限制到 $B$。

## Recognition Cues｜识别线索

Pairing, dice, and lottery questions often hide whether outcomes are ordered and whether trials are independent. 看到‘随机、给定、至少一次’要分别判断样本空间、条件和补集。

## Worked-use note｜使用提醒

Use complements for ‘at least one’, and justify equal likelihood before counting favorable outcomes.
