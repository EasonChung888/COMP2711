# Conditional Probability - 条件概率

## Definition｜定义

$P(A\mid B)$ measures the fraction of $B$ outcomes also in $A$, namely $P(A\cap B)/P(B)$. 条件发生后，分母不再是原样本空间，而是条件事件。

## Recognition Cues｜识别线索

Phrases ‘given that’, ‘assuming already’, and ‘conditional probability’ require a conditioned sample space. 出现‘已知/在…条件下’应先删去不符合条件的结果。

## Worked-use note｜使用提醒

For matching after one fixed pair, count only the remaining matchings; do not reuse the unconditional denominator.
