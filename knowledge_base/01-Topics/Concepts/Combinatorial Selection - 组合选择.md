# Combinatorial Selection - 组合选择

## Definition｜定义

A combination selects an unordered subset: $\binom nk=n!/[k!(n-k)!]$. 组合选择只关心选了谁，不关心挑选次序。

## Recognition Cues｜识别线索

Words such as ‘choose’, ‘committee’, and ‘subset’ signal combinations; a ballot or egg set is not an ordering. 看到‘选择、委员会、子集’先判断顺序是否被记录。

## Worked-use note｜使用提醒

For colour constraints, divide by feasible colour counts or use a complement; make the cases disjoint before adding.
