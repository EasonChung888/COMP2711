# Recurrence Relations - 递推关系

## Definition｜定义

A recurrence defines a sequence from previous terms and initial conditions. 递推关系必须连同初值才唯一确定数列。

## Recognition Cues｜识别线索

State-transition probabilities and parity-counting strings produce first-order non-homogeneous recurrences. 看到 ‘after one more step’ 就按上一状态分类。

## Worked-use note｜使用提醒

Condition on all previous states to derive a transition recurrence, then solve its geometric sum and verify the stated initial value.
