# Modular Congruence - 同余

## Definition｜定义

$a\equiv b\pmod m$ means $m\mid(a-b)$; $a\bmod m$ is the unique remainder in $[0,m)$. 同余记录整除差，余数必须有明确的范围。

## Recognition Cues｜识别线索

Nested moduli, simultaneous congruences, inverses, and divisibility proofs all signal modular reasoning. 看到 $\bmod$ 时先区分等号、同余和代表元。

## Worked-use note｜使用提醒

For uniqueness, subtract two alleged solutions; for a composite modulus, check compatibility modulo the gcd before applying CRT.
