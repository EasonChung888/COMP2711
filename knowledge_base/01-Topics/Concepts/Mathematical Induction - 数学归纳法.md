# Mathematical Induction - 数学归纳法

## Definition｜定义

Induction proves a statement on integers by base case(s) and an implication from established smaller cases to the next case. 数学归纳法由基例和归纳步骤组成，强归纳可使用所有较小实例。

## Recognition Cues｜识别线索

Divisibility with exponent $n$ and existence of quotient–remainder representations are common induction targets. 当递归步需要 $m-n$ 而不只 $m-1$ 时使用强归纳。

## Worked-use note｜使用提醒

State the exact induction hypothesis and substitute it at the correct index; base cases must cover every value not reachable from the step.
