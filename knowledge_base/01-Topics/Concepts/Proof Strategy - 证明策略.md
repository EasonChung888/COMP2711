# Proof Strategy - 证明策略

## Definition｜定义

A direct proof derives a claim from its hypotheses, contraposition proves $\neg Q\Rightarrow\neg P$, and contradiction derives impossibility from a negated claim. 证明方法应匹配命题的逻辑结构。

## Recognition Cues｜识别线索

Irrationality often uses a lowest-terms contradiction; a conclusion ‘$m$ even or $n$ even’ invites contraposition. 先选能把否定条件转成可计算代数形式的方法。

## Worked-use note｜使用提醒

Name the assumption and the contradiction precisely; do not merely state that a contradiction exists.
